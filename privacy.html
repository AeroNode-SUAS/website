Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20th, 2026 • AeroNode SUAS

Overview

AeroNode ("we," "our," or "us") is a student-led robotics and aeronautics team based in Bellevue, WA. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this simple policy. This webpage explains what basic information is collected when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

  • Web Analytics: We use Google Analytics and Firebase to count visitor page views and understand basic traffic patterns (e.g., browser type, approximate country location, pages visited). All analytics data is anonymized.
  • Local Preferences: We store small pieces of data locally in your browser (localStorage) to save your preference choices, such as dark/light display mode and cookie consent choice.
  • No Personal Selling: We do not collect passwords, sell personal data, or run third-party advertising trackers.

Cookies

Web cookies, also known as HTTP cookies, browser cookies, or cookies, are data created by webpages about each user that is stored either locally or on another computer. Our website uses essential local storage and standard analytics cookies to ensure smooth navigation and site performance. Some user data may be transmitted to third parties including Google to track web traffic. Unless logged into our website, your information is anonymized when stored with third parties.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy or our website, please visit our Contact Page.
